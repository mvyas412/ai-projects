## Before you join

MM-RAG is an invitation-only learning pilot, not a production service. It may have
planned or unplanned downtime, and support is best effort within one business day.
Do not upload secrets, regulated data, or information you are not authorized to use.

## What the pilot does

The application stores documents you upload and processes them to answer questions with
source citations. Access is limited by your authenticated workspace permissions. The
pilot operator may pause or revoke access to protect security, privacy, data integrity,
capacity, recovery, or the zero-dollar infrastructure boundary.

## Evidence and feedback

Pilot reports contain aggregate, content-free operational and task-completion evidence.
They do not contain raw documents, prompts, answers, identity values, provider
identifiers, credentials, or tokens. Optional feedback is limited to ratings in
usability, answer relevance, evidence clarity, performance, and accessibility.

Aggregate pilot evidence is retained for 30 days after the pilot closes, then removed
through a reviewed manual operation. Automatic deletion is disabled. Product records
and uploaded content remain governed by the existing workspace lifecycle controls and
are not automatically deleted by pilot closure.

## Your choices

Participation and structured feedback are voluntary. You may stop participating or ask
the workspace Owner to revoke access, review the applicable account data, or initiate a
governed deletion request. Withdrawal does not bypass legal holds, integrity safeguards,
or already-authorized aggregate reporting.

## Consent statement

By choosing to participate, you confirm that you understand the learning-pilot limits,
will upload only authorized non-sensitive material, and consent to the processing and
aggregate evidence described above. Do not proceed if you do not agree.
