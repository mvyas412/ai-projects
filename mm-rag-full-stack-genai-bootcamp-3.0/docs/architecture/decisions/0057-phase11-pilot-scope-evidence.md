# ADR 0057: Phase 11 pilot scope and evidence boundary

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-20
- Milestone: 11.0

## Context

Phase 10 proves the platform can be operated and recovered, but it does not prove that
new users can understand and use it safely without developer guidance.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Advantages | Costs and risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stop at Phase 10 | No new exposure | User usefulness remains unmeasured |
| Public launch | Broad feedback | Premature abuse, privacy, support, and cost risk |
| Invitation-only pilot | Bounded real-user evidence | Requires explicit governance and support |

## Recommendation

Define Phase 11 as a reversible, invitation-only learning pilot for at most ten
registered users. Keep the USD 0 target and content-free aggregate evidence. Do not make
a production-SLA or public-launch claim.

## Accepted decision

The pilot is invitation-only with ten registered users as a hard maximum. Safety,
authorization, privacy, integrity, cost, and recovery gates must pass completely.
Exact live user-success thresholds remain a separately reviewed rollout value; the
initial implementation is limited to content-free synthetic rehearsal.
